Our story

Rooted in Tenkasi

Buying land should feel as clear as standing on it.

We are a real estate team based in Tenkasi, at the foot of the Western Ghats. We help families buy and sell residential plots, farm land, coconut groves and hill estates in Tenkasi, Tirunelveli, Virudhunagar, Thoothukudi, Kodaikanal, Ooty and Kerala.

We do one thing the same way every time: we go to the land, walk the boundary and film it honestly — the approach road, the water, the neighbours and the view. Those walkthroughs now reach a community of 3L+ people on Instagram and YouTube, and buyers from across Tamil Nadu, Kerala and beyond find their land with us.
